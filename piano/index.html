Piano dynamics

A small diffusion model trained on 678 classical performances in (n)ASAP. It rewrites key velocity only — pitch, rhythm, and note lengths stay exactly as in your file. The examples use sample seed 2, the same draw as the public demo.

Try your score

Upload a deadpan piano export from notation software (flat velocities are fine). Processing runs on a CPU server; a long piece can take up to a minute after a cold start.

  • Only .mid / .midi files, max 512 KB and 4,000 notes.
  • Non-drum tracks are merged into one line; percussion is ignored.
  • Best on written scores. A performance recording may sound odd — timing is not changed.
  • Classical piano only; no pedaling, rubato, or duration changes in this model.

Three threads I keep coming back to: expressive piano, text, and small cultural apps.

Piano · listening page

Dynamics from real performances

A diffusion model trained on hundreds of aligned classical recordings. It learns how pianists shape volume while leaving pitch and rhythm alone.

Listen to examples, compare mechanical vs humanized scores, and upload your own flat MIDI when the API is connected.

Open the piano page ↗
Technical notes +

Examples include an original piece (Perpetuity) and openings of Chopin and Liszt works. The model is a compact diffusion transformer on (n)ASAP velocity alignments.

Language · PyTorch

A model shaped by my reading

A small Llama-style transformer trained from scratch on books and essays I care about — Russell, Bourdieu, Hofstadter, Wilde, and my own writing.

Interactive chat is still on the way.

Technical notes +

~25M parameters, BPE tokenizer, RoPE, GQA, RMSNorm, SwiGLU. Trained on roughly 212M tokens from 1,941 texts.
